About the researcher

Mary Ukam

PhD researcher, Henley Business School, University of Reading, UK

Mary Ukam is a PhD researcher in the Department of Digitalisation, Marketing and Entrepreneurship at Henley Business School, University of Reading. She has over a decade of professional experience managing international development, education, innovation and entrepreneurship programmes across Africa.

Her research explores how institutional frameworks, entrepreneurial ecosystem arrangements and individual factors shape women's access to and participation in high-growth entrepreneurship in Nigeria.

Motivated by her professional experience and commitment to inclusive economic development, Mary seeks to understand not only the barriers women face but also how they navigate entrepreneurial ecosystems and access opportunities, recognising their diverse circumstances and entrepreneurial pathways. Her research aims to inform more inclusive policies and targeted ecosystem support strategies that respond to the differentiated needs of women entrepreneurs.
