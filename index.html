PhD study

How do women entrepreneurs enter, participate in and grow businesses in Nigeria's high-growth sectors?

I am studying how rules, regulations, expectations and business support shape women entrepreneurs' opportunities to enter, participate in and grow businesses in Nigeria's high-growth sectors. If you have built, led or tried to build a growth-oriented venture, or you support or shape these sectors, I would like to hear from you.

Who can participate

Who I would like to hear from

Women entrepreneurs

You are or have been a founder, co-founder, owner, co-owner, chief executive or senior strategic leader of a growth-oriented venture based in Nigeria, including ventures that were delayed, blocked, closed or sold.

Find out more about participating as a woman entrepreneur →

Ecosystem actors

You directly allocate, enable or influence resources and opportunities for ventures, for example through investment, lending, incubation, acceleration, mentorship, networks, procurement, market access or sector associations.

Find out more about participating as an ecosystem actor →

Policy and sector informants

You have direct knowledge of policy design, implementation, regulation, sector strategy, public enterprise support or coordination affecting growth-oriented ventures in Nigeria.

Find out more about participating as a policy or sector informant →

What participating in this research involves

  1. Express interest

    A short form that takes under two minutes.

  2. Complete a pre-screening questionnaire

    It takes about 5 to 7 minutes and helps identify the most relevant interview questions for you.

  3. Take part in an interview

    A structured conversation guided by a few open questions, lasting 45 to 90 minutes, online or in person. It is audio recorded only with your permission.

Interested in participating?

Participating in this research is voluntary. Expressing interest is not consent and does not commit you to anything.
