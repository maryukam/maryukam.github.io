Participate in this research

I am inviting women entrepreneurs, ecosystem actors and policy or sector informants to participate in this study. Choose the group that best describes you to see what the interview would cover.

Women entrepreneursEcosystem actorsPolicy and sector informants

Women entrepreneurs

Share your experience of access and participation

I am seeking women who have built, led, owned or tried to establish a growth-oriented venture in Nigeria. I am interested in a wide range of experiences, not only success stories, including ventures that were delayed, blocked, closed or sold.

The interview covers your experience of access to finance, markets, networks, support and regulation, and how you navigated them. It usually takes 45 to 90 minutes.

Ecosystem actors

Contribute an ecosystem practice perspective

I am seeking people who allocate, enable or influence resources and opportunities for ventures in Nigeria's high-growth sectors, for example investors, lenders, incubators, accelerators, mentors and sector associations.

The interview covers how ventures are found, assessed and supported, and how these processes may affect women differently. You will not be asked for confidential client or portfolio information. It usually takes 45 to 90 minutes.

Policy and sector informants

Contribute a policy or sector perspective

I am seeking people with direct knowledge of policy, regulation, public enterprise support or sector strategy affecting growth-oriented ventures in Nigeria.

The interview covers how rules and programmes affect entry, finance and growth, and where gaps arise. You may speak in a personal professional capacity. It usually takes 45 to 90 minutes.
