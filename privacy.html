Privacy and data

How your information is handled if you express interest or participate.

In brief

  • The website itself does not collect your personal information, and it uses no tracking cookies.
  • If you express interest, your details go to me through a University of Reading-approved survey platform (JISC Online Surveys) or my university email. They are used only to contact you about this study.
  • The University of Reading is responsible for protecting your personal information.

What information the study may collect

The study may collect your name, gender and contact details for recruitment and scheduling and to describe the people who took part; screening information about eligibility, role, sector, location and relevant experience; the audio recording and transcript of the interview; and optional background information used to understand variation among participants. You may choose "prefer not to say" for optional questions.

Contact details, consent records and research data are kept separately. Research files use a participant code rather than your name.

How your data is stored and kept

All data is anonymised, kept confidential and securely stored on the researcher's University of Reading OneDrive, and is never shared with anyone outside the research team. Information that identifies you will be destroyed after four years. Anonymised research data may be preserved and made available for others to consult and reuse.

Who is responsible for your data

The University of Reading is the Data Controller. The University collects, analyses, uses, shares and retains personal data for the purposes of research in the public interest, on the lawful basis of a public task in the public interest, where this is necessary for scientific or historical research purposes.

Your rights

You have certain rights under data protection law, although some restrictions apply where data is used for research. You may:

  • withdraw your consent, for example if you opted in to a participant register;
  • access your personal data or ask for a copy;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • ask for your details to be removed;
  • restrict uses of your data; and
  • object to uses of your data, for example retention after you have withdrawn from a study.

Questions or complaints

Contact the University Data Protection Officer at imps@reading.ac.uk, or write to: University of Reading, Information Management & Policy Services, Whiteknights House, Pepper Lane, Whiteknights, Reading, RG6 6UR, UK.

You can find out more about your rights, or make a complaint, through the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Please contact the University Data Protection Officer first.
