Thank you for helping to share this study. To protect people's freedom to choose and their confidentiality, please forward the invitation unchanged to people who may meet the criteria. Please do not send me anyone's name or contact details without that person's permission, and do not ask anyone to tell you whether they responded.

Please follow these safeguards

  • Make clear that participating is voluntary and unrelated to employment, funding, investment, programme access, membership, regulation or other benefits.
  • Invite interested people to contact me directly.

Text you can forward

You are invited to consider a University of Reading PhD study on women's differentiated access to and participation in selected high-growth sectors in Nigeria. The study is seeking women entrepreneurs, ecosystem practitioners, and policy or sector informants with direct relevant experience. Participation is voluntary. If you are interested, please contact Mary Ukam directly at mary.ukam@pgr.reading.ac.uk or visit this study's website. Please do not send confidential information in your first message. The researcher will provide a short eligibility questionnaire and the study information.

PhD research at Henley Business School, University of Reading, UK.

This is an independent doctoral research study. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any conference, organisation or programme.

This study has received a favourable ethical approval from the Ethics Office, Henley Business School, University of Reading.

Contact: mary.ukam@pgr.reading.ac.uk
